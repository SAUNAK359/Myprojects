Prioritize a clear subject, believable exposure, stable framing and natural color. Protect important highlights and faces. Remove no content and avoid extreme zoom. Use exposure compensation conservatively; focus only when the intended subject location is clear. Prefer a subtle natural or warm/cool adjustment over a dramatic grade. Reject recapture when the reference is already strong or only a mild look is needed. On correction, address one dominant issue at a time.
