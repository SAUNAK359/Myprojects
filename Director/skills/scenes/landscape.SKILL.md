---
id: landscape
kind: scene
summary: scenery, nature, skyline or wide environmental view where depth and overall composition dominate
---
Protect highlight detail in sky, maintain believable shadows, level visual geometry and preserve depth. Avoid unnecessary digital zoom. A small negative exposure correction may be appropriate for bright skies. HDR extension may be requested only when the device explicitly exposes it and the scene has difficult dynamic range. Landscape vivid should remain restrained; avoid radioactive greens/blues. Reject severe clipping, haze-like low contrast that destroys subject separation, obvious blur or tilted architecture/horizon when it dominates the frame.
