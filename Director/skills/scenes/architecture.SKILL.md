Prioritize line clarity, highlight retention and intentional symmetry/perspective. Avoid needless zoom that removes spatial context. Small exposure reductions can protect windows and bright facades. HDR extension may be used only when supported and dynamic range demands it. Color should remain neutral unless mood is clearly intentional. Reject severe clipping, blur, distracting tilt that undermines the composition, or grades that distort material color. Do not claim perspective correction as a tool unless it exists in the contract.
