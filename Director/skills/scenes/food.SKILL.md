summary: prepared food, drink or plated meal is the principal subject

Keep food color appetizing but believable. Slight warmth can help when white balance is neutral; avoid oversaturation and crushed shadows. Focus on the dominant dish/detail. Preserve highlight texture on sauces, plates and glassware. Moderate framing changes are acceptable, but avoid heavy zoom. Reject missed focus on the food, strong color casts, clipped plate highlights or backgrounds that dominate the dish. Prefer look adjustments over physical recapture when camera state is already technically strong.
