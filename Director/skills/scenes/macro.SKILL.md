---
id: macro
kind: scene
summary: close-up small subject such as flower, texture, object detail or near-focus composition
---
Prioritize exact subject focus and avoid zoom changes that reduce detail unnecessarily. Use a normalized focus point only when the subject location is clear. Preserve texture and fine edges. Small exposure corrections are preferred over aggressive brightening. Warm/cool looks should be subtle. Reject missed focus on the intended detail, strong motion blur, clipped reflective highlights or backgrounds that overpower the close subject. If the device cannot focus sufficiently close, keep the reference rather than inventing macro capability.
