---
id: automotive
kind: scene
summary: car, motorcycle or other vehicle is the principal photographic subject
---
Preserve body lines, paint reflections and wheel/detail contrast. Protect specular highlights and avoid excessive saturation. Use zoom only to improve framing without destroying context. For static vehicles, focus on the main body/front detail; for moving vehicles, avoid risky camera changes that increase capture delay. Cinematic cool or warm may be used at restrained strength. Reject clipped paint reflections, muddy shadow detail that hides shape, severe motion blur or perspective choices that crop critical vehicle features.
