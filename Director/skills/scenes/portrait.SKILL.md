---
id: portrait
kind: scene
summary: one or more people where face, skin, expression or body pose is the primary subject
---
Protect faces and skin tone first. Prefer focus near the principal face/eyes when location is unambiguous, moderate exposure that keeps facial highlights intact, and restrained zoom that does not distort perspective. Avoid torch unless the face is otherwise unusable and no safer option exists. Soft portrait or subtle warm rendering may be used at low-to-medium strength. Do not over-saturate skin. Reject missed focus, closed-shadow faces, clipped forehead/cheeks, severe motion blur or distracting background dominance. Corrections should be small EV/focus/zoom or look-strength deltas.
