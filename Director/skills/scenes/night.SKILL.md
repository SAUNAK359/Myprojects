id: night
kind: scene
summary: low-light, evening or night scene with dark ambient exposure or bright point lights
---
Preserve the atmosphere instead of forcing daylight brightness. Protect neon, lamps and signs from clipping. Prefer the device Night extension when available and when a recapture is justified; otherwise keep exposure changes conservative. Torch is normally wrong for distant scenes and may flatten mood. Avoid strong cool/warm grades that exaggerate noise. Reject catastrophic blur, black-frame failure, blown light sources that erase the subject, or color casts that make skin/neutral surfaces implausible.
