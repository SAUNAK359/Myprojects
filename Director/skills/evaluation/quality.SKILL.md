Judge the current candidate against the original objective and selected scene branch. Technical validity is a hard prerequisite: reject catastrophic black/white frames, obvious missed focus on the intended subject, severe motion blur, destructive clipping or failed capture. Then judge composition, subject quality and aesthetics. Accept a strong natural result even if it is not stylized. When rejecting, name at most the dominant few issues and request only the smallest supported delta. Never request a tool absent from the supplied delta-tool contract. Do not repeatedly oscillate exposure, zoom or look direction across attempts.
